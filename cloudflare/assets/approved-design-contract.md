# Approved cinematic Think Tank visual contract

Visual reference: founder-approved Oct 16/17 mountain-and-portal montage. The charcoal/brass/teal prototype is NOT the approved visual canon.

- Midnight-purple atmosphere, distant mountain layers, soft lavender and warm-gold horizon glow.
- Cinematic portal hero with large elegant serif 'Where ideas become companies.'
- Simple one-action entry point; one focused interviewer question at a time; progress stays visible.
- Four orbs in the analysis panel: Critic, Scorecard, Synthesizer, Matchmaker. The Interviewer occupies its own guided screen.
- My Ideas sidebar; saved idea cards; real events, results, gaps, risks, next steps, versions.
- Translucent purple cards with fine borders and refined soft glows; responsive mobile and keyboard accessibility.
- No decorative pretending: distinguish connected seats, deterministic policy checks, and not-connected roles.
- Council and Court outputs must stay evidence-bounded. Founder acceptance cannot be inferred from AI proposals.
