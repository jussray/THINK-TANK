Before you begin

A little direction.

  1. Describe what you want to build.

    The interview asks who pays, what they use today, why they would switch and what makes your idea hard to copy.

  2. Explore four perspectives.

    Critic, Scorecard, Synthesizer and Matchmaker use fixed rules over your answers. Their feedback is a starting point, not market validation.

  3. Keep your progress.

    Your Idea Record stays in this browser. Export a copy from the workspace to keep a portable backup; clearing browser storage can remove local records.
