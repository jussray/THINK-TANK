What this page shows. Every screen above is the V0 founder path: enter an idea, answer four questions, run the Critic, Scorecard, Synthesizer and Matchmaker over one Idea Record, save a version receipt, and continue the same record through later pivots. Your Idea Record stays in your browser and exports as a portable file you own. The four roles are deterministic passes over your own answers — a structured heuristic, not external market validation or proof of demand. Accounts, billing, team seats and API access are not part of this version. The sample idea, scores and hub entries above are illustrations, not records. The sign-in and create-account pages are disabled previews of accounts this version does not have.
