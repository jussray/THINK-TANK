What this page shows. Every screen above is the founder path: enter an idea, answer four questions, run the Critic, Scorecard, Synthesizer and Matchmaker over one Idea Record, save a version receipt, and continue the same record through later pivots. Your Idea Record stays in your browser and exports as a portable file you own. The four guides are deterministic passes over your own answers — a structured heuristic, not external market validation or proof of demand. Accounts, billing, team seats and API access are not part of this version: the sign-in and create-account pages simply open this same workspace, and nothing typed there is sent or stored. The sample idea, score and hub entries above are illustrations, not your records.
