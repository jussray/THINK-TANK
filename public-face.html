Think Tank · Public product definition

Your idea sits down for the hard questions.

Think Tank turns a founder thought into a durable Idea Record, challenges the weak assumptions, and scores a fixed first-pass rubric while keeping external validation as a separate gate.

Direct answer

Think Tank is a local-first idea interview system. It asks about the buyer, current alternatives, switching triggers, and defensibility, then preserves the answers and pivots instead of letting the idea dissolve into chat history.

Five roles, one record

Interviewer collects claims. Critic challenges weak assumptions. Scorecard applies a fixed heuristic. Synthesizer turns answers into the record. Matchmaker names a strategic pattern to test next.

Truth boundary

The V0 scorecard is a structured heuristic based on founder-provided answers. It is not external market validation, customer research, investment advice, or proof of demand.

A coherent idea record can sharpen the next experiment. It cannot convert an assumption into evidence by itself.
